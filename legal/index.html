Before relying on a provision

This collection supports research. None of its sources has completed every legal verification step.

01

Check the exact wording

Compare quotations with the original PDF. Dhivehi extraction may contain incorrect reading order or missing characters.

02

Confirm the applicable version

Find all amendments and effective dates for the event date. A recently generated PDF does not establish current law.

03

Read the complete provision

Read related clauses, definitions, exceptions and schedules. An amendment document is not a complete consolidated Act.

04

Connect law and evidence

Check jurisdiction, procedures, applicable judgments and evidence. Keep allegations separate from established facts.
